Quick answers

  • The game is slow or choppy. Close other apps and try again. It runs best on phones from the last few years.
  • My best score and unlocked walkers vanished. They are saved on your device. Deleting the app or clearing the website's data clears them, and they don't move between phones.
  • There's no sound. Check the phone isn't on silent, then tap the ON/OFF buttons at the top right of the game. Music and sound effects have a switch each.
  • I want my name off the leaderboard. Email us with the name and we will remove it.
  • A name is offensive. Tap the little flag next to it on the Top 10. It disappears for you straight away and we get a note.
